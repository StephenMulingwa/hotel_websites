Getting Here is Easy

Simple, step-by-step directions through Beverly Hills

Arrive via Sunset Boulevard

From major freeways, follow signs toward Beverly Hills and continue along Sunset Boulevard toward the hotel district.

Head toward the gardens

Turn onto the landscaped access drive leading to our garden terraces and event grounds. Stay on the main guest road.

Follow Signs

Follow signs for Beverly Hills Hotel. Guest services can guide you if you need assistance finding the entrance.

Main Gate Arrival

Proceed to our main gate. Security and valet will guide you to parking and your event area.
